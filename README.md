#### Hypothesis Testing Summary

**Business question:** Do average sales per transaction differ between male and female customers?

- **Null hypothesis (H₀):** The population mean sales amounts are equal.
- **Alternative hypothesis (H₁):** The population mean sales amounts differ.
- **Test:** Welch's independent two-sample t-test.
- **Significance level:** 0.05.
- **Male average sales:** ₹141,807.34 (511 records).
- **Female average sales:** ₹136,883.21 (489 records).
- **p-value:** 0.4950.
- **95% confidence interval for the male-minus-female mean difference:** −₹9,231.58 to ₹19,079.85.

**Conclusion:** Because the p-value is greater than 0.05 and the confidence interval includes zero, the null hypothesis is not rejected. The analysis does not provide sufficient evidence of a statistically significant difference in average sales between the two groups.

This conclusion applies to the analyzed dataset and does not establish that the population means are identical or that gender causes differences in sales.
